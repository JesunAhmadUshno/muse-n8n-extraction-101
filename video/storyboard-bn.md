# STORYBOARD (BN): "Muse Potential Extraction 101" (Bangla production)
Production: muse-n8n-extraction-101-2026-10-19 | Board version: BN board v1 | Status: for Jesun storyboard gate. NO GENERATION before the gate.
Script: script-bn.md (locked to this board). This board is independent of storyboard-en.md: different hook staging, different presenter blocking, different proof-surface order (order sheet, bridge blueprint, phase sheet, failure sheet as flashback, access sheet), and a boarded light-state device for the flashback. Shared locks only: the set, the character hold, the facts, the brand kit, the Pattern 19 principles.

## SET HOLD
Same environment as the EN production: Jesun's studio desk set from the 2026-10-18 production. Ink Black room (#0B0B0C), desk, wall screen as proof surface, one key light from the upper left, Signal Red (#FF3D2E) the only accent. Geometry never changes. One boarded variation belongs to this film only: the flashback scene (Scene 5) plays at a dimmer board state of the SAME key light (light level changes, direction never flips), and Scene 6 restores full state. The set is re-stilled fresh for this production; zero cross-version reuse of pictures, including from the EN film of this same production. The two films share locks, never frames.

## CHARACTER HOLD
One character: the locked 3D master avatar (avatar-main.png), same face, hair, outfit as the EN film and the 2026-10-18 production. Every still is generated from that single reference, face-gated against the master, every clip drift-gated (start, middle, end) plus the v1.6 temporal gates (4 fps strips, expression sampling, eyes-closed under about 8 percent).
Presenter beats in this film: 9. Generation budget: 6 successful generations per round. Plan: Round 1 generates 6 clips with two declared folds (SH02+SH03 one clip, two windows: phone set down, then the lean-in; SH05+SH07 one standing clip, two windows: hand raised to the dark screen, then the tap-and-turn with the blueprint lit by composite), each window drift-gated on its own frames. Round 2 covers the remaining beat (SH15) plus drift failures.

## CAMERA LAW (Pattern 19)
Static framing only, no camera moves; cuts on sentence boundaries from the animatic word timings. Bangla narration paces slower per word than the EN cut, so this board's shots run a touch longer on presenter beats and the document shots carry more of the runtime. Timecodes are plan estimates.
